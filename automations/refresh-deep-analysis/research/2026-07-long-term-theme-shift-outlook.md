## Part 2 — Ten long-term shifts

### 2.1 The cycle question: 2026 is behaving like a dampened fourth cycle

- The Oct 6, 2025 top ($126,080) landed ~18 months post-halving — squarely in the 12–18-month window where all three prior cycles topped ([Fidelity](https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/trading-investing/four-year-bitcoin-and-crypto-cycles), [Caleb & Brown](https://calebandbrown.com/blog/is-bitcoins-four-year-cycle-broken/)). The 2026 drawdown (−54% at the June $57.7K low) is *tracking the old script with a smaller amplitude*: prior bears fell ≥77%; cycle timing implies a bottom window around **Oct 2026** ([FXStreet](https://www.fxstreet.com/cryptocurrencies/news/bitcoin-four-year-cycle-btc-risks-75-drawdown-with-four-months-of-bear-market-still-ahead-202606291041), [crypto.news](https://crypto.news/bitcoin-price-prediction-four-year-cycle-dead-2026/)).
- What changed is the *transmission*: ETFs, corporate balance sheets, options markets and Fed policy now dominate what miners-and-retail once did. The 2028 halving (subsidy → 1.5625 BTC) is a materially smaller supply shock ([BeInCrypto](https://beincrypto.com/learn/bitcoin-halving-cycle-2028/)).
- **Dashboard implication:** the analyst memory implicitly assumes THESIS-003's "cyclical vs structural" question resolves on flows and macro. Add the cycle-clock as explicit context: a −77% repeat implies ~$29K (no current evidence path); a dampened cycle (−50–60%, bottoming H2 2026) is what the June low + record-low IV are already sketching. **Candidate long-window watch:** "BTC's June 2026 low ($57.7K) holds as the cycle low through Q1 2027" — a directly scorable cycle-structure bet.

### 2.2 Identity re-rating: the digital-gold test failed; the tech-sleeve test is being written

- 2026 delivered the first clean experiment: gold at record highs (~$4,000+) while BTC sat ~49% below ATH; the BTC/gold ratio hit its **most oversold reading on record**; BTC's rolling correlation to U.S. tech ran as high as ~0.8 while its gold correlation fell toward zero ([CME OpenMarkets](https://www.cmegroup.com/openmarkets/economics/2026/Is-Bitcoins-Digital-Gold-Narrative-Losing-Its-Shine.html), [crypto.news](https://crypto.news/bitcoin-trading-like-tech-stock-not-gold/), [FXEmpire](https://www.fxempire.com/forecasts/article/bitcoin-now-most-oversold-versus-gold-on-record-can-btc-still-retake-100k-1581421)).
- Two live resolutions: (a) **permanent re-rating** to a high-beta tech-adjacent sleeve — caps allocator sizing, makes AI-capex cycles BTC's dominant beta; (b) **mean reversion** — Delphi notes comparable BTC/gold troughs historically preceded very large recoveries (~160% average post-rally after ~62% ratio drawdowns) ([CryptoRank](https://cryptorank.io/news/feed/53659-bitcoin-most-oversold-level-against-gold-history)). The deciding evidence would be BTC catching a *hedge* bid in a dollar/fiscal stress event while equities fall — an event 2026 has not yet supplied.
- **Dashboard implication:** this IS the deep question under THESIS-003 and the memory's opportunity-cost-rotation pattern. The owed BTC/Nasdaq + BTC/SOXX systematic read should be upgraded from a TODO to a standing quarterly exercise. The Thesis tab's long-term framing (time-preference, hard money) eventually needs the 2026 gold-divergence acknowledged honestly — it is the strongest counter-evidence yet to the store-of-value narrative *on this horizon* (out of scope for refresh automations; a user editorial decision).

- The survival playbook is pivoting from capital-raising vehicle to operating business: Tether proposed merging Twenty One with Strike + a mining operation to attach revenue to the treasury ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/opinion/2026/03/17/facing-a-crisis-bitcoin-treasury-companies-need-to-pivot-to-survive)). 2026–27 = consolidation, M&A, and discounted-NAV supply risk (a below-NAV treasury company is a rational *seller* or acquisition target, not a bidder).
- This is the memory's `nav-premium → forced-selling` and `financing-window-compression` edges playing out at *cohort* scale — Strategy's $3B cash reserve and BSTR's failed listing are early chapters, not anomalies.
- **Dashboard implication:** migrate `themes/corporate-treasuries.md` framing from "accumulation wave / float compression" toward "shakeout: who survives, who consolidates, what happens to the BTC of those who don't." **Candidate watches:** first ≥10K-BTC treasury-company distressed sale or acquisition (probability-taggable, by mid-2027); Strategy preferred-dividend sustainability through the mNAV<1 regime. ### 2.7 The sovereign layer's real theme is durability — and it's on the election clock

- **Dashboard implication:** when the sovereign theme re-enters the top-5, its frame should be **codification-vs-revocation risk**, not accumulation tallies. **Watch candidates (dated, probability-taggable):** SBR language in the FY2028 NDAA (late 2026); crypto-policy balance post-midterms (Nov 3, 2026).
