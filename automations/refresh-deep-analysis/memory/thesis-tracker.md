## Hygiene rules

1. **Every `DEEP_ANALYSIS.primaryAnalysis` is logged here.** No primary thesis bypasses the tracker.
2. **Falsification conditions must be concrete and observable.**
3. **Probability is updated every run.** Even if it doesn't move, write the same number with a 1-line "no new evidence" note.
4. **Stalled-run counter increments when no new material evidence affects the thesis.** At 4 runs, auto-flag for retirement consideration.
5. **Retirement requires a stated trigger.** Name the specific falsification condition that fired.
6. **Theses don't compete.** Multiple active theses are fine.
7. **A falsifier is a wrongness test, not a noise test (SEP 13).** Cascade/volatility conditions are written downside-only or round-trip-conditioned.
8. **Mechanism amendments are logged, not hidden (OCT 7 / OCT 16).** When the thesis's named driver fails its test but no falsifier fires, write the amendment in the probability note and keep the letters unchanged mid-flight.
