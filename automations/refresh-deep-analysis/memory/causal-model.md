## Hygiene rules

1. **Edges are directional.**
2. **Strength downgrades with contradicting evidence.** A `strong` edge that fails once becomes `medium`; two failures retire it.
3. **Mechanism > correlation.**
4. **Bias toward fewer, sharper edges.** 15–25 well-validated edges is a model.
5. **Cite evidence with run dates or external citations.**
6. **Condense, don't delete (SEP 13).** When an edge's evidence block exceeds ~1 KB, summarize the arc and leave the per-run text to git.
7. **Do not promote a one-session pattern to a fortnight's frame (OCT 16).** Name an edge after a single datapoint at `weak`, not `medium`.
8. **Name the marginal narrative before applying a flow edge (OCT 24).** `fed-rate-cut-signaling → flows` and `risk-asset-relative-performance → flows` predict opposite signs on a day like Oct 22; decide which narrative the tape is trading first. NOV 9: Nov 5 (equity) and Nov 6 (Fed) were each sign-consistent once the marginal narrative was named — the rule works day by day.
9. **A shock crosses a falsifier on secondary data only with the caveat stated (NOV 2).** Liquidation totals are CoinGlass via press; thesis bookkeeping may accept three agreeing outlets, a Brier row may not.
10. **An issuer's valve is gated by the issuer's own measure (NOV 9).** Strategy issued at mnav.com 1.16× while a share-count reading said 0.86×; model the valve on the measure the issuer acts on.
