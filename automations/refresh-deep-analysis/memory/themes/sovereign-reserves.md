## Analytical frame

- The bill converts an executive-order reserve (reversible by the next president) into a statutory 20-year lock — the `federal-commodity-classification → sovereign-reserve-durability` edge's premise, reached by a different statute than CLARITY. Durability without market-structure law stays the open question (the custodian-enforcement unwind path).
- No purchase authority: the bill removes ~328K coins from potential government sale; it adds no demand. Pair with the corporate no-sale behaviour (Strategy) as "buyer-class convergence on scarce float" — supply removed by statute rather than by balance sheet.
- Shared-resource risk (legislation → floor time): the lame duck carries the Dec 11 CR, the NDAA and three crypto bills. A bill can slip on timing alone (the CLARITY July precedent).
