## Current state

Senate Banking Committee advanced the CLARITY Act on May 14, 2026 in a 15–9 markup vote. Two Democrats (Gallego, Alsobrooks) crossed; commodity classification preserved; the Van Hollen ethics amendment (officials-can't-hold-crypto) failed 11–13 and is the largest unresolved fight heading to the Senate floor. **Senate floor vote now expected in early June 2026; White House targeting July 4 signing.** Floor cloture requires 60 votes — ~7 Democrats needed; ethics-language attachment is the math-determining variable. Bill then to House conference. Splits regulatory jurisdiction between SEC (securities) and CFTC (commodities); designates Bitcoin as a commodity. Most consequential U.S. crypto legislation since spot ETF approval order.
