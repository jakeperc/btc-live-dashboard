## Key dated facts

- **JUL 15, 2026** — **Final passage.** FIEA amendments reclassify ~105 tokens (BTC, ETH, XRP among them) as financial instruments. Provisions: crypto insider-trading ban; mandatory issuer disclosures; FSA oversight expanded; penalties for unregistered firms up to 10 years. Effective fiscal 2027. Opens the path for spot Bitcoin ETFs on the Tokyo Stock Exchange — JPX targeting listings in 2027 (some estimates late 2027–2028 for trading). Companion tax reform would cut the top rate on crypto gains from 55% to a flat 20% (matching equities) from Jan 1, 2028 — the tax change still needs its own passage via the FY2027 tax-reform process.
