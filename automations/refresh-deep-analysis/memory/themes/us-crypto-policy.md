## Key dated facts

- **AUG 18, 2026 — SEC proposes Regulation Crypto Assets (33-11434), issued in a surprise announcement after a scheduled open meeting was postponed.** Contents: startup exemption up to **$5M over 4 years**; fundraising exemption up to **$75M per 12 months** (with financial statements + reporting); **conditional safe harbor** — tokens can exit the securities definition once the issuer's promised managerial efforts are complete/abandoned; **preemption of conflicting state registration rules**; **60-day comment period** from Federal Register publication (ends ~mid-late Oct — pin the exact date next run). Analytical frame: mostly de-risks TOKEN fundraising, not BTC directly (BTC was never a security) — the BTC value is second-order: the regulation-by-lawsuit era ends by rule, and the administrative branch builds whether or not CLARITY passes. A rule can be litigated/rescinded; a statute cannot (see causal edge `administrative-rulemaking-substitution ↔ legislative-urgency`, weak).
