## Forecasting hygiene rules

1. **Round to 0.05.**
2. **0.50 is a refusal.**
3. **Extremes carry the most info.**
4. **Anchor on base rates first, adjust for specifics.**
5. **Forecast falsification conditions, not just outcomes.**
6. **A live mechanism is never priced below 0.25 (SEP 13).**
7. **Never restate a row on the partial realisation of its own letter (SEP 26).** Exogenous information only.
8. **Name the instrument — and the clock (SEP 26 / OCT 7).**
9. **A binary forecast resolves hit or miss — never partial (OCT 3).**
10. **Split WHETHER from HOW MUCH (OCT 16).** NOV 16: applied to the Fed (25 vs 50bp) — and the corporate HOW MUCH row showed the split must still be priced off the live valve, not last week's size.
11. **A multi-week sustain row is capped where the ledger says, not where the week feels (NOV 2).**
12. **Name the marginal narrative before pricing a restart row (NOV 9).** The event-restart prior is a Fed-marginal prior; an equity-tape week is a sustain row in disguise.
13. **Do not restate an issuer's row on an outside reading of its own valve (NOV 9).** Strategy trades its own mNAV definition; only its filing shows whether the valve is open.
14. **Match a futures-priced DECISION row to the futures, even into the 0.90–1.00 band (NOV 16).** Shading a 97–100% event to 0.75 for "this Fed surprises" is a refusal to use the band; the surprise record is one-directional (hawkish), and this row IS the hawkish outcome.
15. **A price row made ahead of its window pre-names its exogenous restatement trigger (NOV 16).** Otherwise rule 7 freezes a stale number for the whole window (the <$75K row: 0.30 of Brier).
16. **A correlate is not a mechanism (NOV 23).** Heuristics #3 priced Strategy's buying off price-vs-basis for eleven weeks in which basis and the ATM valve happened to agree; the first week they disagreed, the valve won and the rule cost 0.2025. Before pricing a row off a condition, ask whether the actor can observe and act on it — Strategy acts on its ATM window, not on our basis line.
