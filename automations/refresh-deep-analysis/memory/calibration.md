## Forecasting hygiene rules

1. **Round to 0.05.** False precision (`p=0.673`) is noise.
2. **0.50 is a refusal.** Commit or split into sharper sub-questions.
3. **Extremes carry the most info.** A 0.05 or 0.95 that resolves correctly is high-value data.
4. **Anchor on base rates first, adjust for specifics.**
5. **Forecast falsification conditions, not just outcomes.**
6. **A live mechanism is never priced below 0.25 (SEP 13).** Three of the ledger's four worst scores were "unlikely" rows on mechanisms already documented.
7. **Never restate a row on the partial realisation of its own letter (SEP 26).** Exogenous information only. The THESIS probability (tracker) is where realised legs move; the row scores as made.
8. **Name the instrument — and the clock (SEP 26 / OCT 7).** ANPRM / NPRM / final rule; week or day; Friday close; 10 working days vs 90 calendar days.
9. **A binary forecast resolves hit or miss — never partial (OCT 3).** If the letter genuinely cannot decide it, `no-resolution`, excluded from every figure.
10. **Split WHETHER from HOW MUCH (OCT 16).** A row on an actor's decision (buy / publish / adopt) prices off the operative condition; the size of the action is a separate row with its own prior.
