# Watch-List Outcomes

Ledger of every `watchList` item from prior `DEEP_ANALYSIS` runs. **Every item carries a probability** (`p ∈ [0,1]`, rounded to 0.05). Scored once the `window` has elapsed with a Brier score.

**Outcomes:** `hit` (1.0) / `miss` (0.0) / `no-resolution` (the event text genuinely cannot decide it — no Brier, EXCLUDED from every calibration figure). There is no `partial`: a binary forecast either met its own stated threshold or it did not (rule changed OCT 3, 2026).

**Brier per item:** `(p − outcome)²` where outcome ∈ {0, 1}. Lower = better-calibrated.

**Workflow:**
- At start of run: score any unresolved items whose `window` has elapsed (or whose letter is already DETERMINED). Compute Brier per resolved item, then update `calibration.md`.
- At end of run: append the new run's `watchList` items as unresolved with their probabilities.
- Decay: rows older than 26 weeks → archive (move to bottom under `## Archived`).

## Hygiene rules

1. **Every active item has a probability.**
2. **Probabilities rounded to 0.05.**
3. **0.50 is a refusal.**
4. **Outcome assignment requires a primary-source citation** in the lesson cell.
5. **There is no partial credit (OCT 3).** If the event text genuinely cannot decide it, mark `no-resolution`.
6. **After scoring, update `calibration.md`.**
7. **Every rendered scoring letter carries its own gloss (SEP 13).**
8. **Never restate a row on the partial realisation of its own letter (SEP 26).** Exogenous information only; the plain line may say "on today's evidence it looks unlikely / likely" (OCT 16; NOV 9 — the <$75K row rendered at 0.45 with spot below the line).
9. **Name the instrument in a rulemaking row (SEP 26) — and its review clock (OCT 7).**
10. **Never score a row logged after its resolving information was public (OCT 24).**
11. **A secondary-sourced threshold is logged, not scored, unless two independent restatements agree (NOV 2).** NOV 9: the Nov 5 ≥$1B liquidation day cleared this bar (crypto.news $1.08B, Coinotag $1.24B, PANews $1.66B — all CoinGlass) for contagion condition 2; no Brier row rests on it.
12. **A price row names its series (NOV 9).** The Nov 6 "close" was $75,775 on CoinGecko's 00:00 UTC series and "$74,921" in press — the row's letter decides which counts.
