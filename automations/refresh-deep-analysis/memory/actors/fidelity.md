## Snapshot

- **JUL 2, 2026 update:** No confirmed FBTC re-inflow this run — June closed as the worst-ever ETF month and the complex was still net-redeeming; the Jul 2 price bounce came from spot/whale accumulation, NOT a return of the ETF (FBTC/IBIT) bid. So the manager-fragmentation tell has NOT revived (open question #1 still open). FBTC-vs-IBIT divergence watch (Jul 20 window) remains unresolved; verify per-fund July flows against Farside next run. The early-recovery tell to watch is whether FBTC (lowest-friction) draws the first inflows when flows turn, as IBIT did Jun 11–12.
- **Material event JUN 25, 2026 — THE HOLDOUT FLIPPED.** FBTC joined IBIT among the largest daily ETF outflows, ending its run as the complex's sole inflow magnet. For the prior month FBTC was the one fund still drawing capital while BlackRock's IBIT and the other ten funds redeemed — the single data point the cyclical case could point to as "someone is still buying through the lowest-friction wrapper." When the last holdout flips, the "manager fragmentation, not exit" read loses its anchor: it is no longer one venue consolidating demand, it is the whole complex in redemption. This removed the cleanest remaining piece of evidence that the passive base was merely reshuffling rather than leaving, and was a key tell behind THESIS-003 stepping 0.45→0.40.
- **Prior framing (Jun 17–24):** FBTC was the manager-fragmentation tell — on the Jun 17–18 post-FOMC risk-off day, FBTC drew ~$14M of fresh capital while the complex shed $82M and IBIT redeemed (the "Manager fragmentation" cross-current's founding instance). It remained the sole inflow magnet through Jun 24 even as 2026 YTD cumulative flows turned negative.
- FBTC is the #2 U.S. spot BTC ETF by AUM behind IBIT; lower-fee / lowest-friction relative to GBTC. Exact AUM/holdings not re-pulled this run — verify against Farside + Fidelity disclosures next run.

## Why this actor earned a file

The "Manager fragmentation: ETF flows consolidate by fund under stress" cross-current hinged entirely on FBTC's behavior. Its arc — sole inflow magnet (bullish tell) → joined the outflows (the tell retired) — is itself a load-bearing signal distinct from the net-direction story, and it recurred across three runs (Jun 20 founding, Jun 24 persistence, Jun 28 flip). When the one fund attracting capital on stress days stops, the marginal passive allocator is no longer re-engaging anywhere in the complex.
