## Capital-markets reflexivity (model — MAY 20, 2026; REFINED SEP 13)

- High NAV premium → ATM/STRC issuance accretive → BTC buying (May 11–17; Aug 24–30).
- Compressed premium + BTC below cost basis + a dividend date → BTC sold at the margin to service fixed 11.5–12% obligations (Jun 1; Jul 6; Aug 3; Aug 10).
- Mixed (BTC above basis, premium thin) → idle: buybacks of its own preferred from USD Cash, no ATM, no BTC (Sep 8).
- **Falsification of the refined model:** a BTC purchase funded by ATM issuance while MSTR trades at a DISCOUNT to NAV, or a below-cost BTC sale while the ATM is accretive and no dividend is due.
- Index risk (MSCI, Oct 16) acts on the premium from outside the rate regime: deletion → passive selling → premium compression → the valve closes.
