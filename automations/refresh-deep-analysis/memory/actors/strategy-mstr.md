## Capital-markets reflexivity (new model — MAY 20, 2026)

Strategy's accumulation cadence is gated by NAV-premium-to-BTC and STRC preferred-issuance capacity. Provisional structural model:
- High NAV premium → ATM/STRC issuance accretive → fast accumulation cadence.
- Low NAV premium → issuance dilutive → cadence slows or pauses.
- Sustained drawdown → premium compresses → issuance window narrows.
- A capital-markets reflexivity trap is the bear-case kill condition for the corporate-treasury thesis: sustained price drawdown shrinks the issuance window faster than the structural narrative can travel through allocator decks.
- **Falsification of this model:** Strategy continues 10K+/2-week pace through any sustained drawdown >15% from May 20 baseline.
