- Lead: Elon Musk. Second Musk-linked entity to carry BTC publicly (the first being indirect via past Tesla holdings; SpaceX is now the direct, disclosed one).

## Why it matters

- **Legitimization, not float competition.** SpaceX's 18,712 BTC is small versus Strategy's 845,256, so its direct supply impact is minor. The significance is that a Bitcoin treasury now sits inside one of the most-tracked equities in the market — reaching mega-cap equity allocators (index funds, model portfolios, retail brokerage) who would never buy a spot ETF but now hold BTC exposure by association. See the new `cross-current-patterns.md` pattern "Corporate-treasury legitimization via blue-chip listing + dip-buying" and the standing "Disclosure asymmetry: corporate via S-1, sovereign via on-chain forensics" pattern.
- **Disclosure-via-S-1 precedent.** SpaceX's May 18 S-1 was the first time a major private company surfaced BTC holdings through an IPO filing (not an 8-K). The watch item "a SECOND major late-stage IPO (Stripe/OpenAI/Databricks) discloses ≥5,000 BTC" (p=0.35, by Dec 31 2026) tests whether this becomes a pattern. SpaceX's own listing does NOT resolve that item (same company, same 18,712 BTC).
