# Actor: CFTC (Commodity Futures Trading Commission)

- **Slug:** `cftc`
- **Category:** Regulator
- **Role:** Pre-CLARITY: limited Bitcoin derivatives oversight. Post-CLARITY: primary federal regulator for Bitcoin (spot + derivatives) as a commodity.
- **First tracked:** MAY 18, 2026
- **Last updated:** MAY 18, 2026

## Snapshot

- Long-running stance: Bitcoin treated as a commodity (per CFTC v. McDonnell, 2018).
- Resource constraint: CFTC budget historically ~10x smaller than SEC. Major jurisdictional expansion under CLARITY would require funding (key implementation risk).

## Causal links

- ← `themes/clarity-act.md` (gains primary jurisdiction post-passage)
- → spot-market rulemaking (new authority, no existing rulebook for spot crypto-commodities)

## Behavioral patterns

- Historically light-touch on derivatives venues that follow existing futures-market frameworks.
- Pro-innovation stance relative to SEC.
- Commissioner Pham + others have publicly supported clearer crypto rules.

## Open questions

1. **Funding gap.** Can CFTC actually staff up to oversee a multi-trillion-dollar spot market? Statutory expansion without funding = under-regulation by neglect.
2. **Rulebook timeline.** No existing CFTC rules for spot crypto-commodity oversight. Greenfield rulemaking takes years.
3. **Coordination with SEC.** Inevitable joint guidance needed for hybrid products (e.g., a token that's a commodity but the issuer is a securities-law entity).
